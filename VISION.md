# LLM Wiki Karpathy by George Vision

LLM-Wiki-Karpathy-by-George should be a public, data-free starter for running a
markdown wiki as an agent-managed knowledge layer.

## Product Thesis

The reusable pattern is the layer model: raw sources stay raw, the wiki carries
maintained current understanding, and agent docs define how updates happen.
This lets people preserve durable knowledge without dumping private transcripts
or over-building a database too early.

## Goals

- Keep the template generic, source-grounded, and public-safe.
- Make the raw/log/wiki separation obvious to new users.
- Provide practical operating docs, templates, and lightweight scripts.
- Preserve provenance so claims can be traced to source material.

## Non-Goals

- Do not include private examples or personal notes.
- Do not collapse raw artifacts and synthesis pages into one layer.
- Do not turn the starter into a heavy knowledge-management platform.
